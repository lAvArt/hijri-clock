# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Umm al-Qura Hijri calendar popup, cloned from Omarchy's `omarchy.clock`.
- Offline prayer times: 19 methods, 90 cities, custom coordinates, Asr and high-latitude options, per-prayer offsets, Maghrib Hijri rollover.
- Quiet per-prayer reminders with a configurable lead time.
- Bar label placeholders for the Hijri date and the next prayer.
